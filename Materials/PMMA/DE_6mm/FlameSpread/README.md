# Bench-scale Flame Spread Experiments



Experiments were conducted in the horizontal and lateral orientations. Samples measuring 6 mm x 50 mm x 200 mm (thickness × width × length) were tightly fitted into a water-cooled aluminium sample holder. 

The samples were ignited at one end using a hot wire, which was removed after about 60 seconds to allow a self-sustained flame spread. The flame spreads along the sample length in the direction opposite to the cooling water flow.

Two layers of ceramic paper were used to insulate the backface of the sample, while the lateral faces remained in direct contact with the water-cooled holder.

Temperature measurements were taken using K-type thermocouples (diameter of 0.25 mm) which were fused to the sample surface. Four thermocouples were positioned along the centre line of the sample at 60 mm, 90 mm, 120 mm and 150 mm away from the ignition end.
