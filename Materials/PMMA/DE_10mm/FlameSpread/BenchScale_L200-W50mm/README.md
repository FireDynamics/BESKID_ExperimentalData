# Bench-Scale Flame Spread Experiments




Experiments were conducted in the horizontal, lateral and downward orientations, using samples measuring 10 mm x 50 mm x 200 mm (thickness × width × length).

Ignition was achieved using a hot wire, which was put in contact with one of the sample ends and was removed after about 60 seconds to allow a self-sustained flame spread along the sample length.



## Thermocouple Data


For all orientations, samples were tightly fitted into a water-cooled aluminium sample holder, such that the lateral and the rear faces of the sample were kept at isothermal boundary conditions. The flame spread occurs in the direction opposite to the cooling water flow.

In the lateral orientation, an additional experiment using a calcium silicate (CaSiCO3) sample holder was conducted. As with the aluminium holder, the sample was tightly fitted into the holder. In this case, the lateral and rear faces were considered thermally insulated.

Temperature measurements were taken using K-type thermocouples (diameter of 0.25 mm) which were fused to the sample surface. Four thermocouples were positioned along the centre line of the sample at 40 mm, 80 mm, 120 mm and 160 mm away from the ignition end. 



## Heat Release Rate (HRR) and Mass Loss Data



Measurements of heat release rates and mass loss were simultaneously measured using the gas analyser of a Fire Propagation Apparatus (FPA) and a load cell, respectively. Only the lateral and the downward spread were considered. Both orientations were conducted with the water-cooled aluminium sample holder. Surface temperatures were not measured.
